# Glossary

The terms of **Chain of Command**. Each entry links to the section of the [architecture](architecture.md) that defines it; where the two disagree, the architecture is right.

- _**Adaptation**_: a _**playbook**_ rule on the recognized enemy strategy, other _**Beliefs**_, and _**Assessments**_ that changes _**ranks**_ and _**weights**_, or adds and withdraws _**Desires**_. It scales with the recognition's confidence ([4.11](architecture.md#411-strategy-playbooks-and-production)).
- _**Agenda**_: the active _**Intentions**_, ordered by _**rank**_ and then _**weight**_. _**Allocation**_ serves _**needs**_ in this order ([4.6](architecture.md#46-deliberation)).
- _**Allocation**_: the only owner of units and money. It serves _**needs**_ in _**agenda**_ order ([4.8](architecture.md#48-allocation)).
- _**Assessment**_: what _**Beliefs**_ mean for the bot, shared by every part that needs it, such as the _**threat assessment**_, which rates threats to own assets, or the _**danger assessment**_, which rates each region. It is computed after _**Beliefs**_ and, like them, never proposes _**Desires**_ ([4.4](architecture.md#44-assessments)).
- _**Belief model**_: a pure function that owns one section of _**Beliefs**_: it takes the previous section and the _**observations**_, and returns the next section. Only _**belief models**_ revise _**Beliefs**_ ([4.3](architecture.md#43-beliefs-and-knowledge)).
- _**Beliefs**_: the bot's world model, in plain data: what it holds true, has seen, or infers ([4.3](architecture.md#43-beliefs-and-knowledge)).
- _**Combat estimate**_: the _**Knowledge**_ function that measures fighting strength. _**Tactics**_ judge fights with it plus named modifiers, and a _**need**_ may ask for strength measured by it ([4.3](architecture.md#43-beliefs-and-knowledge), [4.9](architecture.md#49-execution)).
- _**Commitment constant**_: one small _**weight**_, the same for every _**Intention**_: the least gain worth moving a unit at all. It is part of the _**switching margin**_ ([4.8](architecture.md#48-allocation)).
- _**Concern**_: something the bot keeps caring about all game, such as safe bases (the _**base-safety concern**_) or the right strategy (the _**strategy concern**_); BDI literature calls it a maintenance goal. It reads _**Beliefs**_ and _**Assessments**_, and proposes a _**Desire**_ when a condition breaks or is about to ([4.5](architecture.md#45-desires)).
- _**Contact**_: a _**Belief**_ about an enemy unit or structure: what was seen, where, when, and how it is known ([4.3](architecture.md#43-beliefs-and-knowledge)).
- _**Decision record**_: what each step of the _**mind**_ returns besides its orders: every decision and why. It is written to log files of its own, and tests read it ([4.14](architecture.md#414-the-decision-record)).
- _**Default owner**_: the _**Intention**_ that receives units nobody asked for: RunEconomy for workers, Reserve for army units and heroes ([4.8](architecture.md#48-allocation)).
- _**Deliberation**_: checks _**Intentions**_' conditions, adopts _**Intentions**_ for valid _**Desires**_, and orders the _**agenda**_ ([4.6](architecture.md#46-deliberation)).
- _**Desire**_: an outcome worth pursuing, backed by one or more _**proposals**_. It commits to nothing, lasts while anyone proposes it, and has at most one _**Intention**_ at a time ([4.5](architecture.md#45-desires)).
- _**Directive**_: what is asked of one unit: a _**task**_, a _**role**_, _**latitude**_, and optionally where to stand and where to fall back ([4.9](architecture.md#49-execution)).
- _**Enemy model**_: the _**Beliefs**_ about the enemy, fed only by _**Perception**_: _**contacts**_, inferred tech, where unseen units probably are, the recognized strategy, the army estimate, and scouting coverage ([4.12](architecture.md#412-the-enemy-model)).
- _**Expectation**_: an _**adaptation**_ that guards the _**playbook**_'s own assumptions, such as "no air units before 8:00". A broken _**expectation**_ runs _**Deliberation**_ early ([4.11](architecture.md#411-strategy-playbooks-and-production)).
- _**Failure policy**_: how an _**Intention kind**_ handles failed _**plans**_: how long a failed _**plan**_ is excluded, how many attempts are allowed, and then whether to recur, retrying later, or to fail, giving up until its _**Desire**_ lapses ([4.7](architecture.md#47-intentions)).
- _**Fingerprint**_: a sign of an enemy strategy in the _**strategy library**_: a small rule over what was seen and when, such as an early Town Hall away from the enemy's start ([4.12](architecture.md#412-the-enemy-model)).
- _**Hierarchy contract**_: the rules between the levels of execution: orders go down as constraints from a closed vocabulary, feedback goes up only as status and through _**Beliefs**_, and units may deviate to survive ([4.9](architecture.md#49-execution)).
- _**Intention**_: a commitment to a _**Desire**_, pursued through _**plans**_ until it is achieved, becomes impossible, or is no longer wanted ([4.7](architecture.md#47-intentions)).
- _**Intention kind**_: the declaration behind _**Intentions**_ of one sort: conditions, _**plans**_, _**requests**_, and a _**failure policy**_ ([4.7](architecture.md#47-intentions)).
- _**Interruption cost**_: what interrupting an _**Intention**_ now would waste, given by name and valued in gold in one shared place. It is part of the _**switching margin**_ ([4.8](architecture.md#48-allocation)).
- _**Judgment**_: an interpretation only one module needs, such as a _**Tactic**_'s view of its own fight. It stays private in that module ([4.4](architecture.md#44-assessments)).
- _**Knowledge**_: game data and the map as they are when the game starts, prepared offline and supplied when the _**mind**_ is created. It never changes during a game. Its pure functions, such as navigation and the _**combat estimate**_, live beside it ([4.3](architecture.md#43-beliefs-and-knowledge)).
- _**Latitude**_: how far a unit may deviate from its _**Directive**_: its retreat threshold, its leash, and its opportunism, none, own, or free ([4.10](architecture.md#410-opportunities-and-latitude)).
- _**Mind**_: all of the bot's reasoning, between _**Perception**_ and the _**order executor**_. There is one per game ([4.1](architecture.md#41-one-mind-two-boundaries)).
- _**Need**_: what an _**Intention**_'s current _**plan run**_ asks _**Allocation**_ for: units, gold, lumber, or food ([4.8](architecture.md#48-allocation)).
- _**Observation**_: what _**Perception**_ reports each tick: own units, visible enemy and neutral units, items on the ground, game time, and what happened this tick ([4.1](architecture.md#41-one-mind-two-boundaries)).
- _**Opening book**_: a _**playbook**_'s exact first steps of the game, in order, such as five Wisps, an Altar of Elders, and scouting the enemy start. The ExecuteOpening _**Intention**_ follows it ([4.11](architecture.md#411-strategy-playbooks-and-production)).
- _**Opportunity**_: a short-lived gain worth a detour, weighed the same way at every level: its value, minus the detour, the risk, and what it interrupts. A _**Unit Behavior**_'s _**opportunities**_ take small ones for its unit, a _**Tactic**_ local ones for its group, and a _**plan**_ those within its _**Intention**_'s goal; larger ones become _**Desires**_, usually ranked _**Opportunity**_, with a _**window**_ ([4.10](architecture.md#410-opportunities-and-latitude)).
- _**Order executor**_: the only code issuing Warcraft orders ([4.1](architecture.md#41-one-mind-two-boundaries)).
- _**Pause condition**_: when an _**Intention**_ must wait for now, such as Creep while all its heroes are dead. While it holds, the _**Intention**_ is paused, not dropped ([4.7](architecture.md#47-intentions)).
- _**Perception**_: the only code reading Warcraft state for decisions. It turns what the bot can see into _**observations**_, filtering by visibility ([4.1](architecture.md#41-one-mind-two-boundaries)).
- _**Plan**_: one way to pursue an _**Intention**_, chosen by its precondition and preference. Each attempt at it is a _**plan run**_ ([4.7](architecture.md#47-intentions)).
- _**Plan run**_: one attempt at a _**plan**_: a small, explicit, re-entrant state machine. It declares its _**Intention**_'s _**needs**_, returns _**Directives**_, and reports its status and its _**interruption cost**_ ([4.7](architecture.md#47-intentions)).
- _**Playbook**_: a strategy as data: phases, an _**opening book**_, production goals, _**adaptations**_, and builds ([4.11](architecture.md#411-strategy-playbooks-and-production)).
- _**Proposal**_: one source backing one outcome: how strongly (a _**rank**_ and a _**weight**_) and why (a reason). _**Proposals**_ for the same outcome merge into one _**Desire**_ ([4.5](architecture.md#45-desires)).
- _**Rank**_: a _**proposal**_'s category, from _**Survival**_ down to _**Background**_: a lethal threat is _**Survival**_ and a serious one _**Emergency**_; the game plan's own goals are _**Game plan**_, and so is a small threat; a short-lived gain is usually an _**Opportunity**_, and upkeep that is never urgent is _**Background**_. A higher _**rank**_ always comes first ([4.5](architecture.md#45-desires)).
- _**Reflex**_: a _**Unit Behavior**_ module that runs first and may override the _**Directive**_ to keep the unit alive ([4.9](architecture.md#49-execution)).
- _**Request**_: a _**Desire**_ an active _**Intention**_ proposes for a goal it depends on but does not pursue itself, such as an item for its hero ([4.7](architecture.md#47-intentions)).
- _**Role**_: how a unit should play its _**task**_, such as Harasser or Tank ([4.9](architecture.md#49-execution)).
- _**Skill**_: a _**Unit Behavior**_ module that uses the unit's abilities and items as its _**role**_ suggests ([4.9](architecture.md#49-execution)).
- _**Starved**_: the progress of an _**Intention**_ whose _**needs**_ go unmet. It keeps its state, and its _**starvation**_ has a named cause ([4.7](architecture.md#47-intentions)).
- _**Strategy library**_: the known enemy strategies, kept as data, each with its _**fingerprints**_ ([4.12](architecture.md#412-the-enemy-model)).
- _**Strategy recognition**_: the inference that matches what was seen against the _**strategy library**_, and gives the likeliest one or two strategies with a confidence ([4.12](architecture.md#412-the-enemy-model)).
- _**Switching margin**_: how much more _**weight**_ a challenger of the same _**rank**_ needs to take a unit from its holder: the _**commitment constant**_ plus the holder's _**interruption cost**_ ([4.8](architecture.md#48-allocation)).
- _**Switching rule**_: when a unit may move between _**Intentions**_: never out of an _**uninterruptible**_ step, always to a higher _**rank**_, and within a _**rank**_ only by the _**switching margin**_ ([4.8](architecture.md#48-allocation)).
- _**Tactic**_: a reusable group controller that _**plans**_ run. It decides its group's fight or flight, targets, and positions, and reports its status ([4.9](architecture.md#49-execution)).
- _**Task**_: what a _**Directive**_ asks the unit to do, from a closed vocabulary such as Move, Engage, Hold, Harvest, or Build. The _**Unit Behavior**_ carries it out with the technique of its unit type ([4.9](architecture.md#49-execution)).
- _**Uninterruptible**_: a step of a _**plan run**_ that must not be interrupted, such as a Town Portal about to take effect or a purchase being made, marked so for a bounded time. No unit moves out of it, and its _**Intention**_ finishes it even if its _**Desire**_ is gone ([4.7](architecture.md#47-intentions)).
- _**Unit Behavior**_: one per own unit. It turns the unit's _**Directive**_ into one order each tick: _**reflexes**_, _**skills**_, _**opportunities**_, _**task**_ ([4.9](architecture.md#49-execution)).
- _**Weight**_: a _**proposal**_'s value within its _**rank**_, in gold: how far pursuing it would move the balance between the two sides. It is computed from _**Beliefs**_ and _**Assessments**_. A _**weight**_ of 0 vetoes the _**proposal**_ ([4.5](architecture.md#45-desires)).
- _**Window**_: how long an opportunity lasts. A _**Desire**_ that can no longer fit its _**window**_ gets _**weight**_ 0 ([4.5](architecture.md#45-desires)).
